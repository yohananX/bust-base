How pricing works

School-wide applies to everyone, Level to a grade band, Class to one class only. Leave term blank for one-time fees (e.g. Registration Pack).

Student type: ALL bills everyone with one row. NEW / RETURNING splits the fee so each group can pay a different amount — the price's type always wins over the category's type. A NEW-only price is invisible to returning students: they use the ALL price if one exists, otherwise they are not billed for that fee at all.
